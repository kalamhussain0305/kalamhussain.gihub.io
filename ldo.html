📘 Chapter - LDO (Low Dropout Regulator)

LDO Mobile Motherboard ka ek bahut important Voltage Regulator hai. Yeh IC alag-alag Components ko Clean aur Stable Voltage provide karta hai.

🎨 LDO Pin Diagram

चित्र: 5-Pin LDO का रंगीन पिन डायग्राम

1. LDO Kya Hota Hai?

LDO (Low Dropout Regulator) ek Linear Voltage Regulator hai. Iska kaam High Voltage ko Low, Stable aur Noise-Free Voltage me convert karna hota hai.

Mobile Motherboard me CPU, RAM, Camera, Display, SIM, Audio jaise IC ko alag-alag Voltage chahiye hota hai. Ye Stable Voltage LDO provide karta hai.

2. LDO Kyun Lagaya Jata Hai?

Battery lagbhag 4.2V deti hai. Lekin Mobile ke har IC ko 4.2V nahi diya ja sakta. Har IC ka apna Voltage Requirement hota hai.

Component Voltage
CPU 0.8V - 1.2V
RAM 1.1V - 1.8V
SIM 1.8V
Camera 2.8V
Display 3.0V

Isi wajah se Motherboard me LDO ka use kiya jata hai.

3. Buck Converter vs LDO

Buck Converter LDO
Efficiency High Efficiency Kam
Current Zyada de sakta hai Current Limited hota hai
Switching Noise hota hai Output bahut Clean hota hai
CPU Main Supply Camera, Audio, SIM jaise Circuit

4. Real Life Example

Sochiye ek badi Water Tank hai.

  • Water Tank = Battery
  • Main Pipe = PMIC
  • Water Regulator = LDO
  • Room = CPU / Camera / SIM

Jaise har Room me alag Pressure chahiye, waise hi Mobile ke har IC ko alag Voltage chahiye. Ye kaam LDO karta hai.

Quick Revision

  • ✔ LDO = Low Dropout Regulator
  • ✔ Stable Voltage deta hai
  • ✔ Noise kam karta hai
  • ✔ Camera, SIM, Audio, Display me use hota hai
  • ✔ PMIC ke sath milkar kaam karta hai

5. 5-Pin LDO Pin Configuration


           _________
 VIN  ----|         |---- VOUT
 EN   ----|   LDO   |
 GND  ----|_________|
Pin Full Name Kaam
VIN Input Voltage PMIC ya Battery se Voltage leta hai.
EN Enable LDO ko ON ya OFF karta hai.
GND Ground Current ka Return Path.
VOUT Output Voltage Stable Voltage IC ko deta hai.
FB* Feedback Output Voltage ko Stable rakhta hai (Har LDO me nahi hota).

6. Har Pin Ko Aasan Example Se Samjhiye

VIN
Jaise Water Tank se Pipe me paani aata hai, waise hi VIN se LDO ke andar Voltage aata hai.

EN (Enable)
Jaise Room ka Switch ON karte hi Light jalti hai, waise hi EN High hote hi LDO Start ho jata hai.

GND
Jaise Paani wapas Drain se nikal jata hai, waise hi Current GND ke through Return hota hai.

VOUT
Ye LDO ka Final Stable Output hota hai. Isi Voltage se CPU, Camera, RAM ya SIM kaam karte hain.

FB (Feedback)
Ye Output Voltage ko Monitor karta hai. Agar Voltage kam ya zyada ho jaye to LDO usko Correct karta hai.

💡 Important Tip

  • VIN hai lekin EN nahi → PMIC ya CPU Enable nahi de raha.
  • VIN aur EN dono hain lekin VOUT nahi → LDO ya Output Line me Problem.
  • VOUT hai lekin Mobile ka Part kaam nahi kar raha → Aage ki Circuit Check karein.

9. Practical Testing (Cold Testing)

Phone OFF Condition

Multimeter ko Diode Mode me rakhiye.

Test Point Normal Fault
VIN → GND Board ke hisab se Diode Value milegi 000 / Beep = Short
VOUT → GND Diode Value milegi 000 / Beep = Short
EN → GND Diode Value milegi 000 / Beep = Short

✔ Agar Multimeter lagatar Beep kare ya 000 dikhaye, to us Line me Short ho sakta hai.

13. Real Repair Example

Case : Camera Open nahi ho raha.

  • Camera LDO ka VIN Check kare.
  • EN Signal Check kare.
  • VOUT Check kare.
  • Agar tino theek hain to Camera Connector ya Camera IC Check kare.

Case : SIM Detect nahi ho rahi.

  • SIM LDO Check kare.
  • SIM VCC Voltage Measure kare.
  • Enable Signal Verify kare.

⚠ Important Repair Tips

  • ❌ Sirf VOUT dekhkar LDO Replace mat kariye.
  • ✔ Sabse pehle VIN Check kariye.
  • ✔ Fir EN Signal Check kariye.
  • ✔ Fir VOUT Check kariye.
  • ✔ Agar Output Short hai to pehle Short Remove kariye.
  • ✔ Bina Measurement ke IC Replace karna sahi Practice nahi hai.

14. Motherboard Me LDO Kaise Pehchane?

Board View aur Schematic me LDO ko kaise dhoondhe:

  • ✔ PMIC ke aas-paas search kare.
  • ✔ Naam jaise LDO, VREG, VDD, VCC dekh sakte hain.
  • ✔ Output line CPU, Camera, SIM, Display ya Audio IC ki taraf jati hai.
  • ✔ VIN line PMIC ya Battery Supply se aati hai.
  • ✔ EN line PMIC ya CPU se aati hai.

16. Common Mistakes

  • ❌ Measurement kiye bina LDO Replace kar dena.
  • ❌ VIN Check kiye bina VOUT dekhna.
  • ❌ EN Signal Ignore kar dena.
  • ❌ Output Short Check na karna.
  • ❌ Sirf IC badal dena, Line Check na karna.

17. Quick Revision

  • ✔ LDO = Low Dropout Regulator
  • ✔ Stable aur Low Noise Voltage deta hai.
  • ✔ VIN = Input Voltage
  • ✔ EN = Enable Signal
  • ✔ VOUT = Stable Output
  • ✔ GND = Return Path
  • ✔ VIN + EN hai lekin VOUT nahi → LDO ya Output Line Fault.
  • ✔ VIN hi nahi → PMIC ya Power Line Check.

19. Key Points

LDO Mobile Motherboard ka ek bahut Important Linear Voltage Regulator hai. Ye PMIC se Input Voltage lekar CPU, RAM, Camera, SIM, Audio aur Display jaise Circuits ko Stable aur Clean Voltage deta hai. Fault Finding me hamesha is Sequence ko Follow kare: VIN → EN → VOUT → Load Isi Rule ko Follow karne se LDO Fault bahut aasani se Diagnose kiya ja sakta hai.
